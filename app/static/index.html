    How to read these patterns
    Rotation measures signed angles around (100, 100). Clockwise / counterclockwise needs at least ⅛ net turn and 60% directional consistency; otherwise it is mixed or unclear. Steps passing within 0.1 of the centre are ignored for direction. Quadrant time counts daily closing points; touching an axis alone is not a crossing.
    Expanding / tightening compares median distance from the centre in the last third with the first third. The change must reach 15% and at least 0.1 indicator units, with at least 6 points. Shorter histories are labelled with their actual coverage. These describe relative indicators, not returns or predictions.
